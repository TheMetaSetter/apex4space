About this map

Orange shapes are full 10 km grid cells. The backend selects a cell when the area's exact polygon covers its center. Border cells can extend outside the area.

The map uses EPSG:6933. Source GeoJSON uses longitude and latitude. Boundaries are from Natural Earth.
